About us

App Odyssey LLC is a small independent app developer based in Wisconsin, USA. Every App Odyssey app is free to use, with no ads, ever. The features that make an app useful stay in the free version. We don't sell your data, we don't need an account, and most of what you create stays on your phone.

Optional Pro upgrades, sold through Google Play, cover extras that cost us money to run, such as map imagery, cloud services and data feeds, and support the ongoing work of keeping every app updated.
